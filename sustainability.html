Four things we do with your clothes

Repair first

38% of what arrives only needs a seam, a zip or a wash. Repair beats recycling on every measure, so it is always the first option.

Mechanical recycling

The rest is shredded to fibre and re-spun without chemical solvents. No virgin cotton has entered our supply chain since 2023.

Plastic-free returns

Donation bags, mailers and tags are paper or cassava starch. The same bag your order arrives in comes back to us with your next donation.

Named planting

Every recovered garment funds one native sapling, tagged to its donor and audited for survival at 12, 24 and 36 months.

Where the trees actually go

Nine restoration sites, all named, all visitable. We publish survival rates per site rather than a single national figure, because the number that hides failures is not worth printing.

How we got here

  1. 2018

    Santra opens as a small clothing label in Pune, buying conventional cotton like everyone else.

  2. 2021

    First take-back bin at the Pune store. 1,900 garments come back in four months and the mill idea starts.

  3. 2023

    We stop buying virgin fibre entirely. Every new piece from this point is made from donated material.

  4. 2024

    One tree per garment becomes a standing promise, with donor names on every sapling and the first survival audit published.

  5. 2026

    1.28 million trees standing, 34 cities served, and doorstep pickup made free for everyone.

  6. 2030

    The target: five million named trees and a million garments recovered every year.

How the loop works

Three steps, no cost to you, and a tree planted at the end of every one.

  1. 1

    You donate

    Book a free doorstep pickup or drop your bag at any Santra counter. Worn, torn or faded is fine — we sort everything by hand.

    Book a pickup
  2. 2

    We renew

    Wearable pieces are cleaned and mended. Everything else is shredded, re-spun into yarn and woven into new fabric at our mill in Panipat.

    See the process
  3. 3

    A tree grows

    One garment, one native sapling, planted under your name with a photograph and GPS pin sent to your account within 30 days.

Every tree carries your name

We don't plant into an anonymous forest. Each sapling is tagged to the person who donated the garment that paid for it, so your grove grows as your wardrobe changes. Sign in any time to see where your trees are standing.

  • Native species only — neem, jamun, mahua, arjun
  • Planted by farming families we pay a fixed wage
  • Survival audited at 12, 24 and 36 months

What each renewal method means

Every product page states which of these four routes the piece went through, and how many donated garments went into it.

Comparison of Santra's four garment renewal methods
Method What happens Fibre recovered Trees per piece Warranty
Remade Shredded to fibre, re-spun into yarn, rewoven into new cloth 92% 2–6 3 years
Repaired Cleaned, restitched, resoled or rezipped and sold as it is 100% 1 2 years
Overdyed Stained or faded pieces redyed with plant dyes to even the colour 100% 1 2 years
Patchwork Cutting-room offcuts pieced into one-of-one garments 88% 1–2 3 years

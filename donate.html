What you can send

Condition genuinely does not matter. Holes, stains and missing buttons all have a route through the mill.

Send it over

  • Shirts, kurtas, dresses, trousers, denim
  • Knitwear, coats, jackets, blazers
  • Saris, dupattas, bed linen, curtains, towels
  • Shoes and bags, even single shoes
  • Torn, stained, shrunk or faded anything

Please keep these back

  • Wet or mould-damaged fabric — it spoils the whole bag
  • Items contaminated with oil, paint or chemicals
  • Medical or PPE textiles
  • Foam-filled mattresses and pillows
  • Anything you might miss in a month

Where your bag goes

  1. Day 0

    Collected

    Our rider weighs the bag at your door and you get a receipt on SMS with the garment count.

  2. Day 2

    Sorted by hand

    Eleven sorters in Pune split the bag by fibre, colour and condition. Roughly 38% can be repaired and resold as it is.

  3. Day 9

    Repaired or shredded

    Wearable pieces go to the mending workshop. The rest is shredded, re-spun and woven into next season's cloth.

  4. Day 30

    Trees in the ground

    Your saplings are planted at one of nine restoration sites and the certificate lands in your account.
